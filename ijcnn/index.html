Abstract

We propose VASTSum, an uncertainty-aware and decoder-aligned learning framework for video summarization. The method predicts probabilistic frame-level importance scores via a variational formulation, explicitly modeling uncertainty from multi-annotator supervision. A soft-min supervision strategy aligns training with plausible human annotation modes rather than a single collapsed consensus. A decoder-aligned stability regularization promotes robustness of knapsack-based keyshot selection — mitigating the training–evaluation mismatch introduced by discrete decoding. VASTSum achieves state-of-the-art rank correlations on SumMe and TVSum benchmarks with efficient single-pass inference.
